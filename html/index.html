About Me

I’m a graphic design student building this portfolio as a home for the work I’ve produced at university: digital pieces, print studies, and motion experiments that sit alongside each other and show how I’m developing as a designer.

Outside the brief, I’m drawn to worldbuilding and creative writing—building places, voices, and histories I can return to—and I care about how emotion shows up in what I make. Whether it’s type, layout, or moving image, I like projects where feeling is legible: tension, warmth, unease, or stillness, communicated clearly enough that someone else can feel it too.
