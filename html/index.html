About Me

I’m a graphic design student at the University of Tennessee building this portfolio as a home for the work I’ve produced and will produce.

Design as a craft has always interested me, as it allows creativity to be channeled in a way that benefits others—allowing voices to be heard and ideas to be shared and developed.

My interest in creative writing has made it natural for me to craft visual worlds that can be returned to. Simultaneously, my aesthetic focuses on how emotion shows up in what I make. Whether it’s type, layout, or moving image, I like projects where feeling is legible: tension, warmth, unease, or stillness, communicated clearly enough that someone else can feel it too. There is nothing more satisfying than being able to allow someone to feel a strong emotion because of my work.
