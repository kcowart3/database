Six-word memoir poster

A short class brief to distill a personal story into exactly six words, then translate that constraint into a single typographic poster. The piece leans on scale, contrast, and a narrow palette so the phrase reads as both headline and image.

Ink and paper texture stay visible in the final so the poster still feels handmade when it’s photographed or pinned up; the crop on this page matches the hero used on the home row.
