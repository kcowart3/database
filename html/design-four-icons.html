Four senses icon grid

For this project, I was tasked with designing a single deliverable collection of four related icons. I chose a theme of the four senses, and created my own distinct visual language for each mark, all of them sharing weight, corner radius, and design rules so the set reads as a family instead of four one-off illustrations.

The hero image is the final print-style layout—vector strokes, flat fills, and the small format to show how they appear at small scale.
