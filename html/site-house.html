House of Multitudes

The following site was built around the original idea within the project brief: a digital zine, with a series of unique pages stored inside. I took the idea and decided to allow my current inspirations to fuel it. This led to the creation of a navigable house—a small haunted floorplan, with typography and imagery tuned for unease rather than traditional marketing.
