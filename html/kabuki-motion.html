Kabukicho postcard

This project asked for a postcard campaign for a real place of our choosing. I picked Kabukicho, the dense entertainment quarter in Shinjuku, Tokyo—neon, narrow lanes, and layered signage compressed into a single night walk. The animated study below distills that energy into a looping graphic; the five panels are social-sized crops used to test type, color, and composition before locking the final card layouts.

Scroll the row of studies, click any panel to open a full-screen viewer, and use the side arrows or keyboard to move through the set. The floating × returns you here.
